## 1.1.1

**`22/08/2026`**  
Some improvements and fixes.

### Additions

- ![Addition](/changelog-icons/additions.svg) **`/caption`** Makes a caption section in an image.
- ![Addition](/changelog-icons/additions.svg) **`/dashboard`** Shows a button for the bot dashboard.
- ![Addition](/changelog-icons/additions.svg) **`/gif`** Converts an image to gif (useful for saving images).

### Improvements

- ![Improvement](/changelog-icons/improvements.svg) **`/edit`** Now it supports more options to get images from messages!

### Fixes

- ![Fix](/changelog-icons/fixes.svg) **`/lockdown`** Changed required permissions from CHANNELS to ROLES.
- ![Fix](/changelog-icons/fixes.svg) **`/unlockdown`** Changed required permissions from CHANNELS to ROLES.
